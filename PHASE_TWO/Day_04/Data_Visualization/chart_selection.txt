Before making any chart, pick the right type:



S.No    Question type	                                    Chart to use

1.      Distribution of one numeric column	                Histogram
2.      Compare a value across categories	                Bar chart
3.      Relationship between two numeric columns	        Scatter plot
4.      Distribution of a numeric column by category	    Boxplot
5.      Trend over time or ordered sequence	                Line chart
6.      Correlation between multiple columns	            Heatmap
