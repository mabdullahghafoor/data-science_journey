The right mental model before any code

Most beginners treat visualization as the last step
 — "I'm done analyzing, now let me make a chart." 
 That's backwards.

Visualization is analysis. 
You make a chart to discover something, 
not to decorate something you already know. 
Every chart you make should answer a specific question. 
If you can't write the question before making the chart, 
you're not ready to make it yet.
